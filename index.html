Research

ICML 2026 · Workshop on Mechanistic Interpretability and Workshop on Combining Theory and Benchmark

Stress tests REVEAL fragile temporal and visual grounding in video-language models

REVEAL is a stress-test framework that evaluates 11 video-language models with five controlled probes. Models fail either because visual signals are never encoded or because model priors override the signals that are. Some models give identical outputs for a real video and for random noise.

Co-author · Read the paper →
